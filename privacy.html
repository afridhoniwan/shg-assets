Privacy Policy

SHG Pin Scheduler, an internal tool of Second Half Guides · Last updated: 25 November 2026

What this app is

SHG Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by Second Half Guides (secondhalfguides.gumroad.com). It publishes Pins that we designed ourselves to our own Pinterest boards, on a schedule. It is not offered to the public, and no other Pinterest users sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

Through the Pinterest API, with permission given by our own account owner through Pinterest's OAuth sign-in, the app:

  • reads our own Pinterest account name and the list of our own boards, so it can choose the right board;
  • creates Pins on our own boards, using images and text we prepared in advance;
  • reads our own recent Pins, so it does not publish the same Pin twice.

The app does not read, collect or store information about other Pinterest users, their Pins, boards, followers or messages.

How data is stored

The Pinterest access token is stored encrypted in our self-hosted automation server (n8n), which only we can access. We never ask for or store a Pinterest password. Pin images and text are kept in our own content repository. We do not sell, share or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and removal

Tokens are replaced when they are refreshed and deleted if we stop using the app. You can remove the app's access at any time in Pinterest under Settings, then Security and logins (or Apps).
